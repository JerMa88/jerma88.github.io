# AI & Machine Learning Course Lecturer
**Governor’s Champion Summer Camp, Southern Methodist University**  
*July 2024 | Dallas, TX*

## Course Overview
Designed and delivered a comprehensive 4-week curriculum covering artificial intelligence and modern machine learning for talented high school scholars.

## Topics Covered
* **Intelligent Agents & Search Algorithms**: State-space search, heuristic evaluation, and graph traversal.
* **Data Preprocessing & Feature Engineering**: Tabular transformations, normalization, and handling missing data with Pandas and NumPy.
* **Supervised Learning**: Linear/logistic regression, decision trees, and classification metrics.
* **Deep Neural Networks**: Multi-layer perceptrons, activation functions, backpropagation intuition, and introductory computer vision.
* Guided students through end-to-end hands-on capstone projects applying ML models to real-world datasets.
