Our collaborative research paper, [Efficient sampling of short protein trajectories with conditional diffusion models](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.5c01089), has been published in the **Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation (ACS)**, Vol. 22, Iss. 1 (2026).

Co-authored with Chen Xiong, Praveen Kandhan, Dong Chen, Eric D. Smith, and Dr. Peng Tao. Check out the open-source implementation in our [PyTAO](https://github.com/JerMa88/PyTAO) repository.
